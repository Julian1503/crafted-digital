**Why this is better for the business than v4:**

**What "not marrying one thing" means in practice:** the headline is a capability ("custom software, AI automation and websites, built properly by one senior engineer"), the sweet spot is a type of organisation (small, regulated, admin-heavy), and the geography is Queensland first, Australia-wide second. ## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The business's website (or lack of one) undersells the business: slow, generic, hard to update, or owned by a third party they can't reach.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Template builders (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress themes) look generic and don't convert; performance and SEO are an afterthought.
- Agencies add layers, account managers and cost; the person you talk to isn't the person who builds.
- Cheap offshore/marketplace developers: inconsistent quality, no ownership, no post-launch support.
- AI site builders (Lovable etc.) get you something fast but leave you with a project you may not control and can't extend.
- DIY: the owner's time is worth more spent on the business.
**What it costs them:** Lost enquiries, a weaker first impression than competitors, hours wasted on manual workflows (enrolments, follow-ups, reminders), dependency on people they can't reach.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of being burned again by a developer; embarrassment that the site doesn't reflect their work; feeling stuck and dependent; uncertainty about what a website "should" cost.

**Secondary:** Template/no-code builders (Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress themes) and AI builders (Lovable) — fall short because the result looks like everyone else's, performs worse, and the owner still has to manage it.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing / relying on Facebook, Instagram or a Google Business Profile only — falls short because there is no owned, credible destination that converts and ranks.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Site is slow, generic or embarrassing; previous developer vanished or holds the keys; manual workflows eating hours; missed enquiries.
**Pull:** Premium look, speed, direct access to a senior engineer who also designs, fixed AUD pricing, weekly visibility, local and Australia-wide.
**Habit:** "The current site sort of works"; fear of the migration effort; comfort with the builder's monthly plan; not knowing what better looks like.
**Anxiety:** Cost overruns, being locked into one person, losing content or SEO in a rebuild, being sold tech they don't understand.
